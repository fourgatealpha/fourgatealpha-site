Four dimensions, one system

Most tactical models compare price to one moving average and go all in or all out. 4D asks four different questions about the market and grades its conviction by how many agree.

Graduation comes from agreement across dimensions, not from distance to a single line. That is what lets the model reduce exposure early in a deteriorating market without abandoning a healthy one on a noisy week.

  • Long-term price regimeWhere does price sit within its long-term range? The slowest gate and the last to change its mind.
  • Slow momentumIs the trend still gaining or losing ground over the past several months?
  • Trend qualityIs the move orderly and persistent, or choppy and indecisive?
  • Volume pressureIs money flowing with the trend or leaning against it?
