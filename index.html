BEFORE YOU JOIN

A few useful answers.

What does joining the list cost?

Nothing. Joining the email list does not purchase a subscription. Founding members will receive the first year at half price when the subscription service launches.

Can I explore the systems before signing up?

Yes. The ETF and Stock Picker dashboards are available to explore, including current model information and historical evidence.

Are these results from a live managed account?

No. The performance displays are historical simulations and model results. They are not subscriber account returns; real execution, cash, and share rounding can produce different outcomes.

Does Four Gate Alpha manage my portfolio?

No. Four Gate Alpha publishes general research and model signals. It does not provide personalized portfolio advice or execute orders through this website.
