A weekly system for changing markets.

Four Gate Alpha develops systematic ETF allocation and stock-selection models. The 4D framework combines four dimensions of market behavior: price regime, slow momentum, trend quality and volume pressure.

Our aim is a clear weekly decision supported by a record you can examine. The ETF System adapts market exposure; the Stock Picker adds ranked companies and five allocation tiers. Both use the same market-state framework and three staggered sleeves.
